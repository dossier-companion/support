What Dossier does

A tour of the app, chapter by chapter · for version 1.0

Dossier is a planning desk for Arknights players, on iPad and iPhone. You tell it which upgrades you want for your operators, and it works out the materials you need and where to farm them. Around that it keeps a recruitment calculator, a news timeline for your server, and two small calculators for sanity and pulls.

It is free, has no ads and needs no account. Everything you enter stays on your device.

How the app is laid out

Dossier has seven chapters. Each one is a list and a detail pane.

  • iPad in landscape: the chapter rail, the list and the detail sit side by side. Drag the divider between the list and the detail to resize them.
  • iPhone, and iPad in portrait: the three are stacked as layers. Swipe right to slide the detail away and show the list, and right again to reach the chapter rail. Swipe left to go back.

Operators

Every operator in the game, with skills, branch traits and modules.

  • Search by name, or filter by branch, faction and the Limited operator tag.
  • Tap E2, a skill's mastery (S1–S3) or a module (X, Y, α) to mark it as a goal. Tap again to remove it.
  • Open a module to read what each of its three stages changes. A second module can be opened beside it to compare.
  • Total materials adds up what the goals on that operator cost.
  • Mark operators as favourites to keep them close.

Materials

One page per material, showing every way to get it:

  • the running event and its stages,
  • an event shop that sells it, if one is open or about to open,
  • the best main-story stage, picked by sanity per drop from your drop rates,
  • the workshop recipe.

Stages & Loot

The current event's stages, the next event's, and every main-story stage that drops a regular material.

Dossier does not ship with a drop-rate table. Each rate is a box you can type a percentage into, or you can fill them all at once: Settings → On-device data → Fetch downloads rates from Penguin Statistics for the server you pick. A fetched stage shows the site, server and date it came from, and typing over a fetched rate marks it as edited.

Recruit

A recruitment tag calculator. Pick a tag you rolled and Dossier lists the combinations with it that can give a higher-star operator. The recruitment pool is kept separately for each server, so the results match what your server can actually give.

News

A timeline of maintenance, events and banners for your server.

  • A banner lists its rate-up operators, each with a button to that operator's page.
  • An event lists what its shop sells without a stock limit.
  • On Global, events and banners that CN has already run appear ahead of time as Predicted, dated from the gap between the two servers. They are replaced once Global announces the real dates.

Planner

All your goals from Operators, added into one shopping list grouped by material tier.

  • Enter how many of each material you own. All owned and Clear owned set the whole list at once.
  • T5 and T4 materials can be broken down into what the workshop needs to craft them.
  • The footer shows what you need, have and still lack, with an estimate of the sanity it will take. The estimate uses your drop rates, and says so when a material has none yet.
  • A material that an open event shop sells without a limit is marked.

Servers

Pick your server on the chapter rail: Global, CN, JP, KR or TW. Global, CN and TW each have their own operators, recruitment pool, events and banners. JP and KR show Global's data. Operators and modules that your server doesn't have yet are tagged with the server that does.

Where the data comes from

WhatSourceWhen
Operators, materials, stages, events, bannersThe game's public data tables, from the ArknightsAssets/ArknightsGamedata repository on GitHubWhen you tap Refresh now, at most once a day
Banner line-ups and event shopsArknights Terra WikiWhen you open the app, at most every 6 hours
Maintenance and event noticesThe game's own notice listsWhen you open the app, at most hourly
Drop ratesPenguin Statistics, or typed in by youOnly when you tap Fetch, at most once a day

The app comes with a copy of the game data, so it works offline from the first launch. It needs a connection only to refresh.

What stays on your device

Your goals, owned materials, drop rates, favourites and settings are saved on your device only. There is no account, no sync and no tracking. Deleting the app deletes them, and they do not move to a new device. The privacy policy has the details.
