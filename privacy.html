Privacy Policy

Dossier for iPad and iPhone · effective 2 November 2026

Dossier does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. This page explains what the app does with data on your device and which servers it contacts.

What stays on your device

Your goals, owned materials, drop rates, server choice, theme, text size and any fonts you add are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Servers the app contacts

To show current game information, Dossier downloads public data. These requests carry no personal information, but like any internet request they reveal your IP address to the server that answers.

  • Game data tables from the public ArknightsAssets/ArknightsGamedata repository on GitHub (api.github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com), with jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) as a mirror. Downloaded when you tap Refresh, at most once a day.
  • In-game notices from the game's public notice lists (ak-webview.arknights.global and ak-webview.hypergryph.com), checked about once an hour while the app is open.
  • Drop rates from Penguin Statistics (penguin-stats.io), only when you tap Fetch in Settings, at most once a day.

Those services are run by third parties under their own privacy policies. Dossier sends them nothing beyond the request itself.

Feedback email

The feedback link in Settings opens a draft in your Mail app. The draft includes the app version, your iOS version, your device model and the game server you picked, so we can reproduce problems. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself, and you can edit or delete that information first. We use emails only to answer you and fix the app.

Children

Dossier is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
