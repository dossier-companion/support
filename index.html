Dossier

A planning desk for Arknights Dokutahs, on iPad and iPhone

Contact

Questions, bugs and ideas: support.dossier@icloud.com. The quickest way is the feedback link in the app (Settings → About), which adds your app version and device to the email so problems are easier to reproduce.

Common questions

Why are the drop rates empty?
Dossier doesn't bundle a drop-rate table. Type the rates you use into Stages & Loot, and the Planner and Materials pick the best stage by sanity per drop.

How do I get the latest operators and events?
Open Settings and tap Refresh now. Dossier reads the game's data tables at most once a day. In-game notices update on their own about every hour.

Can I move my goals to another device?
Not yet. Everything is stored on the device you use, with no account and no sync.
